Analysis runs a 10,000-simulation Monte Carlo model over 30 years of NASA climate data. It takes 15–60 seconds depending on API response times.

How it works

  1. Soil + anomaly — SoilGrids pH, SOC, clay. Current 30-day precipitation vs. 30-year normal.
  2. Seasonal outlook — ENSO phase (La Niña / El Niño / Neutral) + 14-day forecast.
  3. Monte Carlo — 10,000 simulations weighted by ENSO history and current anomaly signal.
  4. Risk scoring — IBM Granite reasons over crop-specific stress data to produce loss estimates.
  5. Hedge recommendation — Monte Carlo yield-correlated put pricing sized to your budget and risk tolerance.
